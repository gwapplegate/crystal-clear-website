About Us

Built on trust, one window at a time.

Crystal Clear Window Cleaning Service Inc. was founded in 1997 by Scott Pine with a simple mission: deliver premium exterior cleaning while treating every property with respect. Nearly 30 years later, that mission hasn't changed.

We show up on time, give honest estimates, and don't consider a job finished until it exceeds expectations — which is how we've built lasting relationships with customers across the Greater Triad.

No job too big or too small.

We work on homes and businesses alike — from a single window on a single-story house to a full exterior refresh on a large commercial property. Residential or commercial, one story or many, every job gets the same careful, professional standard.
