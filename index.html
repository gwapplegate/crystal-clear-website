Our Story

Built on trust, one window at a time.

Crystal Clear Window Cleaning Service Inc. was founded in 1997 by Scott Pine with a simple mission: deliver premium exterior cleaning while treating every home with respect. Nearly 30 years later, that mission hasn't changed.

We show up on time, give honest estimates, and don't consider the job finished until it exceeds expectations — which is how we've built lasting relationships with homeowners across the Greater Triad.
