Privacy Policy

Effective September 9, 2026

Crystal Clear Window Cleaning Service Inc. ("Crystal Clear," "we," "us," or "our") operates windowcleaningnc.com (the "Site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit or interact with our Site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you give us directly. When you call, text, email, or submit a request through our Site, you may share your name, phone number, email address, service address, and details about the job you're requesting an estimate for.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our Site and its third-party tools (described below) may automatically collect certain data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages viewed, and how you interacted with the Site.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to estimate requests and communicate with you about our services;
  • Schedule and carry out the window cleaning, gutter cleaning, or pressure washing services you request;
  • Improve our Site and understand how visitors use it;
  • Measure the performance of our advertising, including ads run on Facebook and Instagram.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our Site uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta (Facebook/Instagram), to help us understand the actions people take on our Site after seeing one of our ads — for example, calling, texting, or emailing us to request an estimate. The Meta Pixel may use cookies and collect information about your browser and device.

Meta may use this data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy. You can learn more about how Meta uses this information, and manage your ad preferences, through your Facebook Ad Preferences settings. You can also opt out of interest-based advertising generally through tools like the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page.

How We Share Information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except: (1) with service providers who help us operate our business and Site (such as hosting and advertising platforms), (2) if required by law, or (3) with your consent.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

Our Site is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
